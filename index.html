UstanoviteljicaFounder

Urška Repušič

Urška je bila dolga leta profesionalna tekmovalna plezalka na najvišji mednarodni ravni. Plezanje jo je naučilo discipline, vztrajnosti in zaupanja v lasten občutek. Danes je slikarka in pesnica, z diplomo iz financ, in vodi REMAKO.

For years Urška was a professional competition climber at the highest international level. Climbing taught her discipline, resilience and trust in her instincts. Today she is a painter and poet with a degree in finance, and she runs REMAKO.

  • Evropska prvakinja v balvanskem plezanju
  • European Champion in bouldering
  • Državna prvakinja v balvanih in hitrostnem plezanju
  • National Champion in bouldering and speed
  • Svetovna mladinska podprvakinja
  • World Youth Vice Champion
  • Stopničke na World Beach Games
  • Podium at the World Beach Games

Njeno umetnost in zgodbo najdeš na byurska.com.
